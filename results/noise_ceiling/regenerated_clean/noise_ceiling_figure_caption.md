# Figure caption — noise ceiling

**Delay-period noise ceiling for five subjects.** Each column is one subject. The top row shows
the distribution of bounded noise-ceiling estimates across 360 Glasser regions. Bins and axis
limits are identical across subjects (0 to 1, bin width 0.05). The vertical line is the median. A red point sits on the zero bin at a height equal to the number of regions with a noise ceiling of exactly 0.
The annotation gives the median, the mean, and that zero count.
Zeros are included in the first bin and are not omitted. Sub-05’s network maps are uniformly at the bottom of the color scale because every network estimate is 0. The middle row paints the same region
values on the left and right lateral cortical surfaces. The bottom row paints the 12
Cole–Anticevic network estimates onto the regions that belong to each network. All maps use one
color scale from 0 (light) to 1 (dark blue). Medial-wall vertices are omitted. The quantity
shown is the bounded Spearman–Brown noise ceiling, obtained after averaging split-half Pearson
correlations in Fisher *z*. It is not the raw split-half correlation. Negative split-half
correlations are not plotted as negative noise ceilings; their frequency is 0.12–0.55 of region
splits and 0.08–0.79 of network splits (see supplementary fraction-negative maps). Estimates use
only unique correct delay betas from 12 task variants, 50 disjoint split-half iterations, and
seed 20260926.
