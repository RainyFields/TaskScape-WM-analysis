# Proposed Figure 8 caption

**Figure 8 | Within- and across-subject spatial-pattern similarity of delay-period activation.** Split-half Pearson correlations of delay-period beta maps at (a) 58,396 cortical vertices, (b) 360 Glasser parcels and (c) 12 Cole–Anticevic networks. For each of the 12 task variants, each participant’s unique correct delay trials were randomly partitioned into two non-overlapping halves, averaged, z-scored across spatial units, and correlated. Small points are the 12 task-level estimates (mean of 50 random split-half iterations). Large circles (within-subject) and squares (across-subject) are family-level means of those task estimates (DMS; 1-Back location/category/identity; InterDMS location/category/identity × ABAB/ABBA; CTXDM LCI/CIL). Within-subject values are the diagonal of the 5 × 5 matrix; across-subject values are the ten unordered participant pairs, symmetrised. The 50 iterations stabilise the estimate; they are not independent biological samples, and split-to-split variability is Monte Carlo variability of the random partition. The same *y*-axis range is used in all panels; negative correlations are not truncated. Source data: `figures/fig8_regenerated_clean/fig8_within_across_similarity_source_data.csv`.
